# Supplier Outreach — Script & Website Pricing Tiers

**Context:** Outreach approach for suppliers identified in the Gauteng Event Professionals & Suppliers Marketplace research doc as having no website found (Section 5 — ~18 businesses, mostly reachable via phone/Facebook only). Two-layer pitch: free TurnoutPro marketplace profile as the hook, paid website build as the upsell/wedge to get them signed up. The website is not a standalone product line — it's purely a door-opener to bring suppliers onto the marketplace.

---

## Outreach Script

**Opening**
"Hi, is this [business name]? This is [your name] from TurnoutPro — we're building a platform connecting corporate event planners across Gauteng with suppliers like you."

**The hook — free profile**
"We'd like to create a free profile for your business on the platform, so organisers can find you and request quotes directly — no cost to you. We'd just need a few photos, a list of your services, and the areas you cover."

**If hesitant**
"There's no catch — it's just a way to get you in front of corporate clients who are looking for suppliers like you."

**The upsell — website**
"I also noticed you don't have a website at the moment — we help suppliers get a proper one built too, starting around R1,000, and we'll work around your budget if that's a stretch."

**Close**
"Would you be open to a quick chat so we can get your profile set up?"

---

## Website Pricing Tiers

| Tier | Price | Includes |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | R1,000 | Single page — logo, photos, services list, WhatsApp/call button, link to TurnoutPro marketplace profile |
| Tier 2 | R1,500 | Up to 3 pages, contact form, link to TurnoutPro marketplace profile |
| Tier 3 | R2,000 | Up to 5 pages, testimonials, pricing display, Google Maps embed, link to TurnoutPro marketplace profile |

Every tier links to the supplier's TurnoutPro marketplace profile. The site exists to bring suppliers onto the marketplace, so the link is never left out.

Pricing is flexible — willing to work around a supplier's budget rather than losing the sign-up over price.

---

## Reminder on priority order

Before contacting the full no-website list, triage first:

- Fill thin categories first (e.g. security, AV, transport) over oversaturated ones (e.g. catering)
- Prefer suppliers with an active Facebook presence over those with no online presence at all — more likely to respond and follow through
- Treat all contact details from the source research doc as unverified until confirmed on the call
